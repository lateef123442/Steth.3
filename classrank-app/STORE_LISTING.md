## App name (30 char limit on iOS)

## Subtitle (iOS, 30 char limit) / Short description (Android, 80 char limit)

## Promotional text (iOS, 170 char limit — can be updated without a new build)

Daily quizzes, fair cross-department rankings, and a streak worth keeping.

## Full description

```
Steth turns studying into something you actually want to do daily.

EVERY DEPARTMENT, ONE LEADERBOARD
Whether you're in Computer Science, Nursing, Law, or Fine Arts, Steth
gives your department its own daily quiz and its own leaderboard — then
rolls everyone up into fair Faculty and Campus-wide rankings. No more
"that comparison doesn't even make sense" — our ranking system normalizes
across departments so a tough major never structurally loses to an easy one.

BUILD A STREAK THAT MEANS SOMETHING
Answer today's quiz to keep your streak alive. Miss a day and it resets —
unless you're a Steth Pro subscriber, in which case Streak Shield gives
you a one-day grace period.

CLIMB THREE LEADERBOARDS
- Department: compete against peers in your own field
- Faculty: compete across related departments, normalized for fairness
- Campus: the whole university, one leaderboard

CONNECT WITH YOUR DEPARTMENT
Share questions, discuss coursework, and celebrate wins in your
department's feed — or post campus-wide.

FOR TEACHERS AND ADMINS TOO
Teachers can publish and manage their department's daily quiz content.
Admins get platform-wide engagement analytics and manage departments and
teacher access.

Download Steth and see where you rank.
```

about UGC specifically.

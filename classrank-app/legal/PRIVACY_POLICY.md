## 1. Who we are

Steth ("we", "us", "our") operates the Steth mobile application (the
"App"), a quiz and ranking platform for university students, teachers, and
administrators.

Contact: [privacy@yourcompany.example] — replace with a real, monitored address.

## 2. What information we collect

**Information you provide directly:**
- Name, email address, and password (for your account)
- University name, department, and year of study (students)
- Any content you submit (quiz questions, if you're a teacher)

**Information collected automatically:**
- Quiz answers and results, points, streaks, and rankings
- Basic app usage data if crash reporting is enabled (device type, OS
  version, and the technical details of any crash — see Section 5)

**What we do not collect:**
- We do not collect precise location data.
- We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and maintain your account
- To calculate and display your points, streak, and leaderboard ranking
- To let teachers publish quizzes and see aggregate (not individual-level
  beyond what's needed for grading) engagement statistics for their
  department
- To let administrators manage departments, teacher accounts, and platform
  operations
- To diagnose and fix bugs (via crash reporting, if enabled)
- To communicate with you about your account (e.g. password reset emails)

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell
your personal information.

## 4. Who we share information with

We use the following service providers ("processors") to operate the App.
Each only receives the data necessary to perform their function:

- **Supabase** (database, authentication, hosting) — stores all account and
  app data described above.
- **Sentry** (crash reporting, if enabled) — receives technical crash
  reports, which may incidentally include parts of app state at the time of
  a crash.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose
information if required by law (e.g. a valid court order).

## 5. Crash reporting

If crash reporting is enabled in this deployment, technical error reports
(not your quiz answers or personal messages) are sent to Sentry to help us
fix bugs. Reports are tagged with an internal user ID, not your name or
email, so we can investigate patterns without directly identifying you in
the crash report itself.

## 6. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you
delete your account (Profile → Delete Account, in-app), your profile,
points, streak, and quiz history are permanently deleted immediately — this
is not a "soft delete" or a grace period.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or
delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.
You can:
- **Delete your account and all associated data** at any time, directly in
  the app (Profile → Delete Account).
- **Request a copy of your data** or ask questions about this policy by
  contacting us at the address in Section 1.

## 8. Children's privacy

Steth is intended for university students, who are generally adults.
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of
digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data
from children in that age range. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including
encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and database-level access controls. No
system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "last
updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be communicated
in-app or by email where required by law.
